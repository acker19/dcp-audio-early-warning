Where this goes

Built for a competition.
Designed for a plant.

Every part of this build maps to a real production equivalent. Being explicit about the gap is what makes the roadmap credible.

Business case

One plant is the proof. Every plant is the point.

Scale

The same node design covers any plant with the same six equipment categories — the model changes per equipment type, not per site.

Cost of inaction

A single day of unplanned kiln downtime already costs more than this entire sensing rollout across a plant.

Data compounds

Every hour of real plant data collected sharpens the model further — the earliest deployments make every later one better.
