TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – ADDITIONAL FAQ

Q: Why is my dashboard showing "No Data Available"?
A: The IoT device may be offline, disconnected from WiFi, improperly configured, or the backend server may not be running.

Q: Why are all sensor readings equal to zero?
A: Possible causes include:

* Sensor disconnection
* Faulty wiring
* Sensor initialization failure
* Incorrect GPIO configuration
* Device firmware issues

Q: Why are sensor readings not changing?
A: The sensor may be frozen, improperly mounted, disconnected, or the machine may not be operating.

Q: Why is the device showing Offline status?
A: The server has not received any data from the device within the expected communication interval.

Q: Why is my device frequently disconnecting?
A: Possible causes include:

* Weak WiFi signal
* Power instability
* Router issues
* Firmware crashes
* Network congestion

Q: Why is the dashboard updating very slowly?
A: Possible causes include:

* Poor internet connection
* High server load
* Large database queries
* Backend performance issues

Q: Why are graph updates delayed?
A: Data processing or communication delays may temporarily slow graph updates.

Q: Why does the dashboard show old timestamps?
A: The device may have lost synchronization, stopped transmitting data, or local buffering may still be uploading older records.

Q: Why are there missing data points in my graphs?
A: Missing data may occur due to:

* Temporary network outages
* Device restarts
* Sensor failures
* Server downtime

Q: Why are my AHI values changing rapidly?
A: Rapid changes may result from:

* Sudden machine faults
* Improper sensor mounting
* Excessive environmental vibration
* Temporary operating condition changes

Q: Why are AHI values always close to 1.0?
A: The machine may be healthy, thresholds may be too lenient, or there may be issues in feature extraction.

Q: Why are AHI values always close to 0?
A: Possible causes include:

* Severe machine faults
* Incorrect calibration
* Sensor malfunction
* Software bugs in calculations

Q: Why are there frequent false alarms?
A: False alarms may occur due to:

* Loose sensor mounting
* Excessive external vibration
* Incorrect thresholds
* Noise contamination

Q: Why does the machine remain in Critical status after maintenance?
A: The system may still be processing old data, thresholds may need recalibration, or the fault may not have been completely resolved.

Q: Why does the system still show alerts after the machine has been repaired?
A: Alerts may remain active until manually acknowledged or until sufficient healthy data is collected.

Q: Why is RMS extremely high?
A: High RMS values may indicate:

* Severe imbalance
* Misalignment
* Mechanical looseness
* Bearing degradation

Q: Why is kurtosis extremely high?
A: High kurtosis often indicates repeated impacts or localized defects such as damaged bearings.

Q: Why is the anomaly score high while RMS appears normal?
A: Some faults generate impulsive events that significantly affect anomaly detection without greatly increasing overall vibration energy.

Q: Why is the anomaly score always zero?
A: Possible causes include:

* Incorrect feature extraction
* Model loading failure
* Software bugs
* Sensor data not being processed correctly

Q: Why are machine status colors incorrect?
A: Alert thresholds or frontend status mappings may be configured incorrectly.

Q: Why is the chatbot giving incorrect answers?
A: The knowledge base may need updating, or the chatbot may not have sufficient context.

Q: Why can't I log into the dashboard?
A: Verify:

* Username
* Password
* Internet connection
* Server availability

Q: Why am I getting authentication errors?
A: Your session may have expired or your API token may be invalid.

Q: Why can't I add a new machine?
A: Possible causes include:

* Missing required information
* Duplicate machine ID
* Insufficient user permissions

Q: Why can't I delete a machine?
A: You may not have administrator privileges.

Q: Why are reports not generating?
A: Possible causes include:

* Backend errors
* Database issues
* Invalid date ranges
* Server overload

Q: Why can't I download reports?
A: Verify your internet connection and ensure report generation has completed successfully.

Q: Why are exported files empty?
A: The selected time range may not contain any available data.

Q: Why is the ESP32 continuously restarting?
A: Possible causes include:

* Insufficient power supply
* Memory issues
* Firmware crashes
* Hardware faults

Q: Why does the ESP32 become hot?
A: Excessive heat may indicate:

* Overvoltage
* Hardware damage
* Excessive processing load
* Short circuits

Q: Why does the sensor become hot?
A: Sensors normally generate very little heat. Excessive heating may indicate hardware damage or wiring problems.

Q: Why is WiFi connection failing?
A: Verify:

* SSID and password
* Signal strength
* Router configuration
* Network availability

Q: Why is MQTT communication failing?
A: Verify:

* Broker address
* Port number
* Authentication credentials
* Internet connectivity

Q: Why are API requests failing?
A: Check:

* Backend server status
* API endpoints
* Authentication tokens
* Firewall settings

Q: Why is the backend server not starting?
A: Possible causes include:

* Missing dependencies
* Incorrect configuration files
* Port conflicts
* Database connection issues

Q: Why is the database connection failing?
A: Verify:

* Database credentials
* Network connectivity
* Database server status

Q: Why is the device sending duplicate data?
A: Duplicate transmission may occur after network interruptions or repeated retries.

Q: Why is memory usage increasing continuously?
A: This may indicate:

* Memory leaks
* Excessive buffering
* Improper data cleanup

Q: Why is CPU usage very high?
A: Possible causes include:

* Excessive computations
* Large numbers of connected devices
* Inefficient backend processing

Q: Why is the device clock incorrect?
A: The device may have failed to synchronize with the network time server.

Q: Why are timestamps incorrect?
A: Incorrect timezone configuration or clock synchronization failures may be responsible.

Q: What should I do if the system completely stops working?
A:

1. Verify power supply.
2. Check sensor connections.
3. Verify network connectivity.
4. Restart the IoT device.
5. Restart backend services.
6. Check server logs.
7. Contact the system administrator if the issue persists.

Q: How can I verify that the entire system is functioning properly?
A:

1. Confirm the device is online.
2. Verify sensor readings are changing.
3. Check that graphs update in real time.
4. Confirm alerts are generated correctly.
5. Test report generation and data export functionality.
